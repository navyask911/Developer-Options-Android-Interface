# Privacy Policy for Developer Options Shortcut

Last updated: October 2026

"Developer Options Shortcut" is developed as an ad-supported utility application. This page informs users regarding policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information.

### Information Collection and Use
Developer Options Shortcut does not directly collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). All system diagnostic information (such as Android version, device model, and USB debugging status) is read locally on the device and is never uploaded to any external server.

### Third-Party Services & Advertising
The app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:
* **Google AdMob / Google Play Services:** Used to serve advertisements. These services may collect device identifiers (such as Advertising ID), IP addresses, and diagnostic crash data in accordance with Google's policies.
  * [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### Security
We value your trust in using our application. Since no sensitive user data or personal records are collected or stored by the app, your personal data remains private on your device.

### Children’s Privacy
This application does not address anyone under the age of 13, nor do we knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.
